# Oney2
Oneyplays randomizer PWA that gets its data from a .CSV file on your device.
Doesn't seem to work on Android, but works ok on desktop Chromium or Chrome.
Once installed, clicking the "open csv" button lets you pick your .CSV and give permission to "Allow on every visit".
See https://developer.chrome.com/blog/persistent-permissions-for-the-file-system-access-api
